# Interpretation

All nine within-model pairs have lower feasible and original-optimal probabilities after replacing intended interactions with geometric interactions. The three mean feasibility changes are 7.8128% to 1.0985%, 21.6069% to 10.5518%, and 36.0506% to 24.0008%. This is a controlled pair-distortion effect for these layouts and this pulse, not proof of a universal limit.

Both balance encodings have infeasible intended ground states on seed 2006. Geometry happens to restore an optimal minimum in its reduced encoding, yet finite-pulse success still decreases. Ground-state correctness and dynamical preparation are distinct. Full one-hot medium and large quantum comparisons remain untested.
